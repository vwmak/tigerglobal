Data Sources & Notes:
All figures represent quarterly unsecured personal loan originations in USD billions sourced from public company filings (10-Q, 10-K, earnings supplements, and investor presentations).

• LendingClub (LC): Data from 2018 Q1 onward. LC transitioned to a bank charter in Feb 2021; figures reflect total personal loan originations.
• Upstart (UPST): IPO December 2020; data available from Q1 2021. Originations include loans facilitated on the platform regardless of who retains them.
• SoFi Technologies (SOFI): SPAC merger June 2021; personal loan originations reported from Q1 2021.
• Oportun (OPRT): Focuses on underserved borrowers; smaller average loan size. Data from Q1 2021.
• OneMain Financial (OMF): Includes personal installment loans (unsecured + secured); figures are for total new receivables originated. Data from Q1 2019.
