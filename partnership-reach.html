Data Sources & Notes:
Audience reach figures are approximate monthly estimates drawn from public sources including company press releases, Comscore, Nielsen, SimilarWeb, SEC filings, and trade publications (The Block, Bloomberg, Reuters, CoinDesk, SportsPro). Where exact cross-platform reach figures are not publicly disclosed, conservative estimates based on available traffic and viewership data are used.

• Exclusivity status is based on the terms described in press releases and news coverage at the time of announcement. Some deals may have evolved since their initial announcement.
• Google Finance reach is not separately tracked; the figure shown reflects estimated Google Finance page views/unique visitors (a fraction of total Google Search traffic) based on SimilarWeb category estimates.
• Robinhood MAU figure used (not total funded accounts) to better represent active engagement.
• Some partnerships (e.g. DraftKings, PrizePicks) are noted as collaborations with limited public reach data and are estimated conservatively.
