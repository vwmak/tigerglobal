Three structural moves that reprice the whole set

None of these is a company Augustus sells against. Each changes what the buyer expects as standard.

Announced Jun 2026 · live H1 2027 The Clearing House deposit token JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and more than a dozen peers are pooling tokenized deposits on one TCH-operated network. It makes 24/7 programmable USD settlement an expectation rather than a differentiator — but only between member banks' own deposits, which is precisely the set of institutions Augustus does not target.
2026 charter cycle The OCC opened the gate Erebor (Feb), Coinbase (Apr), Circle (Jul), World Liberty (Aug) and OpenReserve (Sep) all cleared OCC approval inside eight months, alongside Augustus's own conditional approval in May. The scarce national charter that anchors Augustus's moat is getting less scarce — though full-service charters with deposit powers remain in the single digits.
2026 Distribution bought the rails Stripe took Bridge for $1.1B and Mastercard agreed to take BVNK for up to $1.8B. Stablecoin orchestration is no longer a startup layer Augustus competes with — it is a feature of two networks that already sit in front of every merchant and issuer.

Four things the field said against the thesis

From Tiger's own expert and customer calls. These bear on how much the overlap scores actually matter.

Kraken · expert call Instant settlement needs a closed loop Both counterparties have to bank with Augustus for settlement to be genuinely instant. Until the network has both sides of a corridor, the product is a faster correspondent, not a replacement — which is why the interoperable-network idea with Anchorage and Ripple Bank keeps coming up.
Kraken · expert call Stablecoin settlement is "not game-changing" At current cost structures the stablecoin leg was described as interesting rather than decisive. The pitch that wins is compliance speed and always-on access, not the rail itself.
Fed policy · in flight Skinny master accounts and FedNow A skinny master account plus FedNow and RTP adoption could compress the USD-clearing edge for everyone — the deck's own FedNow series shows quarterly settled volume going from $2bn in 2Q24 to $1.09tn by 1Q26, though still under 10bps of Fedwire.
Multiple calls Complement, not replacement Customers consistently framed Augustus as a complement to their correspondent banks and wanted to see the US entity mature with Tier 1 clients before deepening. That caps near-term wallet share regardless of what rivals do.

How to read the scores

Commercial traction · 0–10
Evidence of paid, live volume: settled volume or revenue, deposits gathered, named institutional clients, corridor coverage. A funding round is not traction; a pre-operational charter scores 1–3 however well capitalised.
Technology buildout · 0–10
How much of the settlement stack runs in production versus sits in a pilot or a filing: own core versus vendor core, direct rail connectivity, whether tokenized settlement is a product or a bolt-on to a legacy mainframe.
Overlap · 0–10
Contest for the same mandate — a non-US or underbanked FI buying always-on USD clearing on a regulated balance sheet. Same buyer and same promise scores 8+; adjacent rails or a different customer scores below 5.
Augustus baseline
Traction 4.0 · Buildout 7.0. Live euro clearing on the legacy Ivy rails with Kraken, Bitpanda and KuCoin and billions of euros a year; USD clearing conditionally chartered in May 2026 but not yet at scale.
