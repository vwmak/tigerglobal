Four structural moves that reprice the set

Not companies Augustus sells against — each changes what the buyer expects as standard.

Aug–Sep 2026 · live Swift's ledger became the neutral layer HSBC and Standard Chartered settled the first live cross-border interbank tokenized deposit on Swift's ledger; Citi followed with FAB and OCBC. Augustus's core claim — a neutral bridge between institutions, versus single-bank walled gardens — is the thing Swift is now supplying to incumbents who never had to build it.
Announced Jun 2026 · live H1 2027 Two bank consortia, one year out 17 banks are pooling tokenized deposits on a Clearing House network tied to RTP and CHIPS, while a separate 21 systemic banks form a shared dollar stablecoin company for a 1H27 launch. Both make 24/7 programmable USD settlement an expectation — and both serve member banks' own customers, not the foreign FIs Augustus targets.
30 Jun 2026 Open USD inverted the float A 140-company consortium — Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, Coinbase, BlackRock, BNY, Standard Chartered, Anchorage and Ripple among them — routes reserve income back to distributors instead of the issuer. Circle fell 12% on the news. It reprices the net-interest half of every stablecoin-adjacent business model, Augustus's included.
2026 charter cycle The OCC opened the gate Erebor, Coinbase, Circle, Mercury, World Liberty, Revolut and OpenReserve all cleared OCC approval inside eight months, alongside Augustus's own conditional approval in May. The scarce charter anchoring the moat is getting less scarce — though full-service charters with deposit powers remain in single digits.

How to read the scores

Commercial traction · 0–10
Paid, live volume: settled volume, revenue, deposits, named clients, corridor coverage. A funding round is not traction — a pre-operational charter scores 1–3 however well capitalised.
Technology buildout · 0–10
How much of the settlement stack runs in production: own core versus vendor, direct rail connectivity, tokenized settlement as a product versus a bolt-on.
Overlap · 0–10
Same buyer and same promise scores 8+; adjacent rails or a different customer scores below 5.
Internal file · deep / partial / none
Depth of Tiger's own diligence, from a sweep of Egnyte, email, attachments and OneNote. None means the read rests entirely on public sources.
